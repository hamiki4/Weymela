# Financial model and ledger specification

## Wallet and reservations

`BusinessWallet` exposes TotalBalance, AvailableBalance, ReservedBalance and a concurrency version. `TotalBalance == AvailableBalance + ReservedBalance`. Deposits are positive append-only wallet credits. `PromotionReservation` records the committed amount and moves funds atomically from Available to Reserved. `CreatorAllocation` is a child commitment bounded by the Promotion's unallocated amount.

## Journal

`FinancialJournal` and immutable `FinancialJournalLine` are append-only, balanced double-entry records. Typical entries debit/credit Merchant Wallet Liability, Promotion Reserve Liability, Creator Payable, Customer Cashback Payable, Platform Revenue, and clearing accounts. Each event has a unique reference and idempotency key; corrections are compensating entries, never edits.

View charge: allocation/reserve decreases by Business charge; Creator payable and Platform revenue receive the configured snapshot amounts. Hybrid sale charge uses the configured percentages and posts Creator, Customer, and Platform lines. Ordinary unused Creator Budget return debits Creator allocation liability and credits Campaign unallocated reserve liability. A return to Business Available is an Admin-authorized exception, not automatic completion behavior. Settlement creates separate platform revenue settlement records.

The Phase 3 [persistence specification](../architecture/PERSISTENCE.md) defines the authoritative journal, operational history projections, explicit precision, concurrency, reconciliation and durable outbox.

## Earnings and payout

Creator earnings aggregate across all allocations and Promotions. A payout candidate pins exactly one effective configured threshold; preparation alone does not deduct or reserve funds. Admin-confirmed external payment deducts that amount and carries the remainder forward. Customer cashback follows the same threshold pattern and is only generated by eligible verified sales. No role sees another role's private balances.
