## 1. Who and what this notice covers

This proposed notice explains how the Weymela V3 service (“Weymela,” “we,” or “the Service”) would collect and use personal data about Customers, Creators, Businesses, Cashiers, Platform Admins, applicants, and people who contact support. It applies to the V3 website, application, account workspaces, promotions, UGC opportunities, notifications, authentication, and related support. The responsible legal entity and geographic scope must be supplied above.

## 2. Personal data we may process

The V3 application is designed to process the following data categories. The Production owner must confirm the live release, integrations, fields, telemetry, and actual data locations before this list is treated as complete.

- **Account and contact data:** name, email address, phone number, user and profile identifiers, email-verification state, role requests, and Business or Creator profile details.
- **Authentication and security data:** Firebase identity identifier, credential-verification state, password/PIN verifier material where applicable, challenge and recovery state, session and device identifiers, security events, access history, and technical request or error logs. We do not need to include passwords, PIN values, verification codes, or custom tokens in this notice’s records or audit logs.
- **Business and Creator information:** organization and public profile details, role applications and review decisions, business locations or coordinates where supplied, Creator profile and audience evidence, TikTok or other social links, and submitted promotional materials.
- **Promotion and content data:** Promotion briefs, eligibility and participation, application and approval history, submitted links, images or videos, verification and review decisions, and related comments or notifications.
- **Financial and operational data:** wallet balances, available and reserved amounts, deposits and payout instructions if enabled, rewards, cashback, sale and reconciliation entries, promotion budgets, payout and settlement status, configuration versions, idempotency records, and audit history. Payment account details and financial records require special access controls.
- **Legal, support, and preference records:** accepted legal-document versions and times, privacy choices and consent evidence where used, support requests and responses, language preference, and account-closure requests.
- **Device and usage data:** necessary session cookies, application activity, IP/network and device data, diagnostics, and security logs as confirmed in the final telemetry inventory. Optional analytics or advertising tracking must be identified separately and must not be assumed by this draft.

The exact fields, sensitive-data categories, sources, and whether each field is mandatory or optional remain subject to owner verification. Do not collect sensitive personal data unless the purpose, lawful basis, and required safeguards are separately approved.

## 3. Purposes and lawful bases

The proposed purposes are to create and secure accounts; verify contact details; manage role applications and profile access; operate Promotions, creator work and reviews; verify attributed views or sales; maintain wallets and financial records; send service and security notices; answer support requests; prevent fraud and abuse; protect the Service; comply with legal duties; and resolve disputes. A purpose may not be added merely because data is technically available.

Before publication, the controller and counsel must map each purpose and data category to a lawful basis recognized in each served jurisdiction. Where consent is relied on, it must be specific, informed, freely given, recorded, and withdrawable through a disclosed process. Optional marketing or analytics consent must be separate from account or contract acceptance. Required fields and the practical result of not supplying them must be identified in the interface.

## 4. Authentication and account protection

Firebase is used for the configured identity and custom-token authentication flow. The Service may send verification or recovery messages through Resend from an approved sender. Firebase and Resend may receive the data needed to provide those functions under their applicable terms. The final provider list, roles, processing locations, transfer safeguards, and contractual arrangements require owner confirmation. Verification codes, passwords, PIN values, private signing keys, and API keys must not be logged or disclosed.

The Service uses role-based access and session controls to limit access to account workspaces. A user should keep credentials and devices secure, sign out of shared devices, and report suspected compromise through [OWNER: approved security contact].

## 5. How data may be shared with third-party providers

We may share necessary data with service providers that host or secure the Service, provide authentication or email delivery, store protected media, or support backups and operations. The exact hosting, database, media-storage, backup, monitoring, and support providers and the countries from which they may access data must be named in the final inventory.

We may disclose data where needed to provide a feature to another user. For example, a Business may see application and work material submitted by a Creator; a Cashier may see checkout details needed for an authorized transaction; and a user may see the public profile or promotion information needed to participate. Private contact information is not made public by default. Disclosure must follow the permission and feature shown in the Service.

We may disclose or preserve information when required by law, a valid legal process, the protection of users, the investigation of suspected fraud or security incidents, or the establishment or defense of legal claims. Any sale of personal data, targeted advertising use, or unrelated disclosure is not authorized by this draft.

## 6. International processing and transfers

Vendors may process data in countries other than the user’s country. Before publication, the owner must identify actual storage and access locations and counsel must confirm that each transfer is lawful and protected by the required safeguards. No transfer location or safeguard is established by this draft. Sensitive-data transfer or localization restrictions must be assessed against applicable law before the related data is processed.

## 7. Retention and account deletion

We would retain each category only for the period or criteria necessary for its disclosed purpose, a lawful obligation, security, a dispute, or financial reconciliation. The final notice must state the applicable periods or criteria separately for account and profile data, verification and session records, user content and media, financial and ledger records, legal acceptance, support, security logs, and backups. **No numeric retention period is approved by this draft.** Owner and counsel must adopt a retention schedule, including how deletions age out of protected backups.

Users may request closure through the in-Service account or role closure flow, or through [OWNER: approved privacy channel]. Closure can disable access and revoke credentials while certain financial, legal, audit, security, or dispute records remain restricted and retained for their approved period. The Service must explain which records cannot be erased immediately and why. Erasure requests will be evaluated against applicable law and any remaining lawful basis; closure does not promise immediate deletion of every record from active systems or backups.

## 8. Privacy choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, a person may have rights to receive information about processing, access and obtain a copy of personal data, correct inaccurate or incomplete data, request erasure or restriction, object to certain processing, receive or transmit eligible data, withdraw consent, and complain to a competent authority. The controller may apply lawful exceptions and must give reasons where required. Requests can be sent to [OWNER: privacy contact and secure request process]. We may ask for proportionate identity verification before disclosing or changing data.

## 9. Security and incidents

The Service is designed with access controls, protected credential handling, restricted administrative operations, encrypted transport, audit records, backups, and private-media boundaries. The owner must verify these controls and avoid promises that security is absolute. We will investigate suspected incidents, contain and remediate them, preserve necessary evidence, and notify affected people or authorities when required by law. The incident contact and legally required notice timing must be approved and operationally assigned.

## 10. Children, automated processing, and external links

The minimum age, any parental authorization, and safeguards for minors must be set by the owner and counsel before launch. Do not enable accounts for an unapproved age group. The Service may use rule-based checks for eligibility, security, abuse prevention, and promotion review. The owner must inventory any automated decision that has a legal or similarly significant effect, explain its logic and consequences where required, and provide an appropriate review path. External links, including TikTok links, are controlled by their respective providers and their own privacy notices.

## 11. Cookies, changes, language, and contact

The Service uses necessary session storage/cookies to keep a user signed in and protect requests. The final inventory must identify any other local storage, analytics, or tracking technology and provide any required controls. We will show the current notice version and effective date and keep prior versions and acceptance evidence. Material changes will be announced or presented for renewed consent where required.
