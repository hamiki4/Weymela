# Production policy publication candidate

These complete English and Amharic texts replace the earlier placeholder drafts
as review candidates. They are **not approved, published, or consent evidence**.
`publication-candidate.json` records the exact file hashes, proposed versions and
unapproved state. It is deliberately not an importable `publication.json`.

The remaining owner decision is to confirm the legal operator/controller name and
service address, and approve the final four texts and their effective date for
publication. Verified information currently establishes the Weymela brand,
Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, and support@weymela.com; it does not establish a registered
legal entity or a specific service address. No entity name, numeric minimum age,
fixed retention period, exclusive dispute venue, regulatory authorization or
approval letter has been invented. The proposed version date is not an effective
date or a claim of approval.

The texts describe the implemented identity/profile model, role approvals,
Business-scoped Cashier enrollment, provider email verification, private phone
aliases, PIN/password protection, manual deposit settlement, profile closure,
financial/audit retention and actual support channel. They do not promise that a
receipt upload credits a wallet or that profile closure erases financial history.

Preparation references include the official Ethiopian Ministry of Justice
[Personal Data Protection Proclamation 1321/2024](https://justice.gov.et/am/law/personal-data-protection-proclamation/),
[Firebase privacy documentation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy),
and [Resend's processing description](https://resend.com/legal/dpa).
The documents describe purposes, privacy requests, retention and provider
processing in plain language. These references do not constitute an owner
approval, a completed provider contract or a legal-review attestation.

After the owner decision, incorporate the confirmed operator details in both
languages, hash the exact final bytes, and prepare a protected approved bundle
using [the supported publication procedure](../../../deployment/V3-UNIFIED-REGISTRATION.md).
That procedure appends policy versions and an actual publication audit event;
it never manufactures user acceptance. Publish and verify all four documents
before changing the existing consent mode to `Required`.

The registration deployment preserves the existing owner-authorized `Deferred`
mode. The single agreement checkbox remains visible, with policy links and an
accurate unavailable state while no policies have been published. It cannot
record acceptance of missing documents. No financial configuration changes are
part of policy publication or this registration correction.
